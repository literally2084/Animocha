# Animocha
An ambitious, open streaming platform for independent animation with absolutely ZERO AI.

# In development
Animocha is currently in early beta development

# What’s so special to come from Animocha?

## NO AI. Strictly and unapologetically.
No exceptions or excuses. Every line of code will be hand-typed. Every UI element will be hand-drawn. Every masterpiece published within Animocha must be manually reviewed by **human** reviewers.

## Extreme freedom and flexibility for creators
Anyone will be able to start their own Animocha channel, and publish their animations from simple and unserious to ambitious and grand. Importantly though, all creators will be able to share their work under five main monetary options: 100% Free to Watch, Free with (High Standard non-AI) advertisements, Pay-per-view, timed rent, or purchasing ownership of a copy of an animation or animated series/movie/etc. 

There will be almost no censorship beyond what mandatory law requires. NSFW and extremely mature content will be taggable with a special label and access can be restricted to certain age groups.

## Privacy
Animocha will not nor will ever ask for any of your personal information beyond your phone number, username, and email address. Privacy, freedom, and sustainability (ability to comfortably earn money from your published animations) are core elements of what makes Animocha a one-of-a-kind digital cafe!

## Quality of Life (QoL)
Channels and users will be able to customize their profiles with custom fonts, colors, and more. Completely for free. 

## Open-source
Every core part of Animocha’s code will be publicly available and usable under the GPLv3.0 license.
